01 — About

More Than
One Sport

Falaniko “Niko” To'o is an Australian multi-sport athlete developing across several competitive sporting environments.

His sporting background includes boxing, American football, rugby and volleyball. Each sport brings different challenges and opportunities to learn.

Being involved in multiple sports has helped shape an approach centred around training, discipline, teamwork and continued development.

Niko's journey is still being built. The focus is on gaining experience, competing, improving skills and continuing to develop as an athlete.
